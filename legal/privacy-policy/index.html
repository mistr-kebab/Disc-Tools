Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

1. Controller

Antonio Schlömer

Contact: info@disc-tools.de

2. Hosting, CDN & Security

The Disc-Tools service — including the website at disc-tools.de and the dashboard at dash.disc-tools.de — is operated on server infrastructure hosted by Hetzner in Helsinki, Finland. Content delivery and DDoS protection are provided through Cloudflare. All connections are encrypted via TLS.

3. Server Logs

Our web server and API log each request including your IP address, timestamp, HTTP method, requested path, HTTP status code, user agent, and any error messages. These logs are automatically rotated and kept for a maximum of 30 days. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — our legitimate interest in detecting and defending against abuse.

4. Cookies and local storage

This site uses a cookie banner that asks for your consent before optional storage. Categories:

  • Essential: Always active. Needed for login and for storing your consent choice itself. Without them the service cannot work.
  • Analytics: Only stored after you accept. Helps us understand how the tools are used. We use a self-hosted instance of Umami Analytics on our own domain (umami.disc-tools.de) for website analytics. Umami is privacy-focused: it does not use cookies, does not collect personal data, and all data is anonymized. No data is shared with third parties.
  • Preferences: Only stored after you accept. Stores favorites and settings on your device.

Favorites are stored in your browser's local storage and only on your device. This is strictly necessary for the favorites feature you explicitly requested. Your consent choice is stored in local storage so the banner is not shown again; you can change your choice at any time via the Cookie settings link in the footer.

Legal basis for optional cookies: your consent, Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR. You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future.

4. Discord login (OAuth2)

You can sign in exclusively with your Discord account via OAuth2. Discord transmits the data covered by the requested scopes (e.g. user ID, username, avatar, email address) to us. We never receive or store your Discord password.

The data is used to identify you on the dashboard and to provide personalized features (for example your favorites). Legal basis: your consent, Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR. You can disconnect your account at any time in the dashboard.

When you use the login, your data is processed by Discord Inc. and its subsidiaries. Please also read Discord's privacy policy at https://discord.com/privacy.

5. Tools and data you submit

Most tools run in your browser. Data you enter (for example a user ID, an invite code or a color) is processed to answer your request and is not stored unless stated otherwise on the tool page.

6. Retention periods

We store personal data only as long as necessary: account data for as long as your account exists, usage data as stated in the section above, and data you ask us to delete is removed within 30 days.

7. Your rights

  • Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR)
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7 (3) GDPR)

You can exercise all rights via info@disc-tools.de or directly in the dashboard (view, export and delete your data).

8. Right to complain

You have the right to complain to a supervisory authority, for example the Federal Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (BfDI), if you believe the processing of your data violates the GDPR.

9. Age

Discord requires users to be at least 13 years old. By using the login you confirm that you meet this requirement.

10. Data security

This website is only available via encrypted HTTPS connections. Access is additionally protected by rate limiting and security headers.

11. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available on this page.
